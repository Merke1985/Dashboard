All figures are editable and recalculate live in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is saved automatically (no browser storage is used). The donut shows your house value split between what's still mortgaged and your equity. Use Export to save a snapshot as a file, Import to load one back later, and Reset to restore the original figures. The Net Worth page can pull each loan part's remaining balance from here with its Sync button — its own House value field is separate and entered independently there.

Each loan part's Remaining/Paid off figures now advance automatically, one month at a time, every time you open this page — no need to check your bank app each month. If you ever correct a figure by hand (say, after checking the bank), that becomes the new starting point and the automatic monthly steps continue from there. This assumes a fixed rate and no extra repayments — when a loan part's fixed-rate period ends, update its rate yourself once you know the new one.
