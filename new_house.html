Home equity agreement current home

Agreement between Maarten and Nicolas on how to split the equity from selling the current home — prepared ahead of the notary conversation for the new purchase.

Rule
First €100,000 → Maarten
The rest of the equity is split 50/50

Background: Maarten originally put in roughly €100,000 of his own savings when buying the current home. Since then you've both contributed to the mortgage and household costs (see Nicolas' contribution log on the Budget plan). On sale, this €100,000 is first set aside as Maarten's private assets; anything above that in the sale proceeds is split evenly — regardless of the exact final sale price. This rule (rather than fixed euro amounts) was chosen on purpose, since the final sale price can still change before the actual transfer.

How the value is set: the home's value is established with a professional valuation (taxatie) — a step that's needed anyway whenever mortgage financing changes hands. If a second opinion is ever useful, the usual approach is for each of you to have your own valuation done and take the average of the two.

Still to do: have this agreement formalized by the notary when buying the new house, in the financing/transfer deed and/or prenuptial agreement.
