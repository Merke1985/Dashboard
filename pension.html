The gap

In 2016 through 2021 I built up no pension through an employer at all, which left the full annual allowance (jaarruimte) for each of those years unused. Unused room can be recovered by paying into a personal pension product (lijfrente) and deducting it from Box 1 income — but only for 10 years back. Each year's room drops out of that window on its own once 10 years have passed, whether or not I've contributed.
